Physics battles, made in code.

Rolliseum makes short-form videos of code-generated physics simulations – balls, arenas and mazes – published on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

Rolliseum Publisher

Rolliseum Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of Rolliseum to upload finished videos to the owner's own channels and pages (Rolliseum and Aphotic). It runs on the owner's computer and uses the official YouTube Data API and Meta Graph API to:

  • upload a video with its title, description and tags,
  • set its visibility (private, unlisted or public),
  • read the channel/page name to confirm the right account is selected.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
