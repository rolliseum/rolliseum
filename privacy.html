Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers Rolliseum Publisher, a private tool used by the owner of Rolliseum to post videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and Facebook/Instagram accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly) for the channel the owner selects at sign-in.
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram): permission to list the owner's Pages, read basic Page and linked Instagram account information, and publish videos to them.

How data is used

Access is used only to upload the owner's own videos and to confirm which account is selected. No analytics, advertising or profiling is performed.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is stored on any server operated by Rolliseum, and no personal data of other users is collected.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Data is sent only to Google and Meta, through their official APIs, to perform the uploads.

Google API Services

Rolliseum Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or Facebook Settings → Business integrations. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer removes all stored data. Deletion requests can be sent to the contact below.
